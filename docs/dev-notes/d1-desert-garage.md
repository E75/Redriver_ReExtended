# Driver 1 desert and garage missions (plan, 2026-10-19)

Goal: the Driver 1 desert missions (dirt track races, practice laps, free drives) and the garage driving test play like the original, with the desert's own car (spoke wheels). Branch `feature/d1-desert-races`, one green commit per part.

## Findings

- City id 6 of the mission scripts is the training level `TRAIN.LEV` (720000 units square, all desert start spots inside it). `FLAT.LEV` is not used by any script and does not parse as a level.
- Scripts 106-116 use city 6, op 110 (`CFG_96264`) picks the track: 0-3 dirt tracks, 4-6 free drive areas.
  - 106-109: race on track 2, 3, 0, 1 with 6, 5, 4, 3 laps (var 62 = laps, var 63 = lap counter), 5 to 12 checkpoints (TARGET0 + WAIT_STATE) per lap, count-up timer, `SPLIT_TIME` per lap, `BEST_TIME` at the end.
  - 110-113: the same four tracks as endless practice (var 62 = 0x7FFFFFFF).
  - 114-116: free drives (cops off, no target), like the garage scripts.
- Scripts 117 / 118 (city id 2, `IVIEW.LEV`): the garage test. The script only waits; the test itself (manoeuvres, time limit) is code in the original executable, not in the script.
- `TRAIN.LEV` holds one player car (car model slot 5), the cities hold four: that is the spoke wheel car.

## Parts

1. Done: city 6 plays in the Training slot (`TRAIN.LEV`), scripts 114-116 convert as free drives ("Desert Drive 1-3"), the menu button "Desert, Garage" lists the desert and garage missions.
2. Done: races and practice laps (106-113, "Desert Race 1-4", "Desert Practice 1-4"). `ConvertRace` turns the checkpoints into a ProcessTarget chain; at the end of a lap the engine function `0x4000021` (mission.c, Driver 1 missions only) clears the lap's checkpoints and returns 1 after the last lap (laps 0 = practice, endless). Practice resets the clock every lap like the original. Not done: the 3-2-1 countdown (D2 scripts cannot wait, only "GO" is shown), split and best times. Tested with a hook that moves the car to the active checkpoint: race 107 completes after 5 laps, practice 110 keeps going.
3. Done: the desert already starts with the only player car of `TRAIN.LEV` (Training now lists 1 player car, not 2). The spoke wheels were missing in every D1 city: the converter swaps the D1 wheel models for the D2 ones (layout of `MangleWheelModels`); `CopyD1Rim` now gives the D2 hub faces (polys 2 and 3) the D1 rim texture of the same wheel model (clean, blurred, hubcap off). Checked from the side (`REDRIVER2_CAMYAW=1024`) in desert drive 114 and Miami mission 1.
4. Done: garage test (script 117) like the original. The test is code of the original game's car park overlay `IVIEW.BIN`; its numbers and texts are read at runtime from `DRIVER1\IVIEW.BIN` (the player copies it from their own disc), never stored in the repo. Without the file the mission stays a free drive.
   - Voice: the original queues mission speech samples 100-107 from lump 2 of `SOUND\VOICES.BLK` (same block and bank layout as the D2 voice file; the original maps the car park mission to that lump). Samples: 5 wrecked, 6 out of time, 7 passed (0-2 hit remarks, 3 and 4 task remarks, not used yet). `D1Garage_LoadSpeech` loads the lump from `DRIVER1\SOUND\VOICES.BLK` as mission bank in `LoadLevelSFX`; the test queues the closing line and, like the original, ends the mission only after it has been spoken. Without the file the test stays silent.
   - Converter: script 117 (city 2 with var 40 set) becomes a loop `FN_D1_GARAGE_TEST (0x4000022), -3, BranchIf`; the engine function runs one frame of the test and ends the mission itself.
   - `Game/C/d1garage.c`: `D1Garage_Load` reads the file (size 17120, each read instruction checked by opcode), `D1Garage_Step(cp)` per frame, `D1Garage_Draw` the task list, `D1Garage_Reset` in `LoadMission`.
   - Read from the file: texts (NUL ended) at 0x28 start, 0x44 passed, 0x64 wrecked, 0x90 out of time, 0xB0 hit a car, 0xC4 paintwork; time limit at 0x3450 (u32, times 60 = frames); six zone sequences of 15 bytes at 0x33F4 (length, score, zones newest first; 0-3 slalom, 4-5 lap); zone columns and bands from the immediates of the instructions at 0x85C/0x860 (column A x start and width), 0x8D0/0x8D4 (column B) and the five band pairs 0x868/0x86C, 0x874/0x880, 0x888/0x894, 0x89C/0x8A8, 0x8B0/0x8BC (z start and depth; bands give zones 5..1 in column A and 6..10 in column B); six parked cars from the instructions at 0x115C + 0x18 k (x), +8 (z), +0xC (heading).
   - Rules (30 Hz frames, speed = wheel speed >> 12 in D1 units, yaw = angular velocity y >> 12): 2 s start pause with the start text; tasks Burnout (wheelspin button, speed >= 51), Handbrake (handbrake button, speed >= 51), Slalom and Lap (zone history matches a sequence either way), 180 (yaw > 250 for 20 frames), 360 (yaw > 300 for 35 frames), Reverse 180 (reversing at speed >= 101, then yaw > 200 for 20 frames), Speed (6 frames above 180, then down to 19 or less), Brake (above 170, then 20 frames of brake held). All nine within the time limit = passed. A hit on a parked car or new damage to the player car counts as a hit (30 frame pause between hits), four hits = wrecked, time up and the car standing still = out of time.
   - HUD: the D2 mission timer counts down (paused flag, the test sets it), all nine task names on the right, grey until done.
   - Speed is the body's forward speed (linear velocity along the heading): the wheel speed jumps to a fixed value while the rear wheels are off the ground.
   - Car park walls: the converter capped an object's cell coverage at 6144 units, so the long wall models (boxes up to about 11000 units from their origin) only collided near their middle and the car drove out of the car park. Models with collision boxes now cover every cell their boxes reach. The camera skips the scenery collision in the car park (its low ceiling pulled the camera in to about 0.8 of the D1 distance).
   - Menu: the Training slot button is "Car park, Desert"; the list shows "Car park" (117) and "Car park Drive" (118) first, then the desert races, practice laps and drives. The level cache of `SetCityType` also compares `gD1DlcGarage`: car park and desert share GameLevel 9, so after a desert mission the car park test started in the desert.
   - Tested with a build-copy hook (scripted pad, teleport through the zones): every task is detected (180 and 360 come from a fast handbrake turn and burnout donuts, Reverse 180 from a J-turn), the test passes with all nine, four hits end it as wrecked, the clock runs out after 60 s and the car stays inside the walls.
